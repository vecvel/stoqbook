# Reporting & Analytics Module

Version: **1.0.0**

---

# Overview

The Reporting & Analytics module provides actionable insights into every aspect of the business.

Unlike operational modules, reports do not create or modify business data. They aggregate information from Products, Inventory, Sales, Purchases, Customers, Suppliers, Employees, Expenses, and other modules to help businesses make informed decisions.

The reporting engine is fully dynamic, permission-based, tenant-aware, and exportable.

# Business Rules

* Reports never modify business data.
* Reports always respect RBAC permissions.
* Reports always respect tenant isolation.
* Reports always apply active module restrictions.
* Hidden columns are excluded from exports.
* Scheduled reports execute with the owner's permissions.
* Financial reports require explicit permission.
* Deleted records are excluded unless requested.
* Cached reports expire automatically.
