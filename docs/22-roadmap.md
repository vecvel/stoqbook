# StoqBook Product Roadmap

Version: **1.0.0**

---

# Vision

StoqBook aims to become a complete Retail & Business Operations Platform rather than just a stock management system.

The long-term vision is to build a modular platform that can scale from a single local shop to multi-national retail enterprises while remaining highly customizable, secure, and easy to use.

---

# Mission

To provide businesses with a single platform to manage

* Inventory
* Sales
* Purchases
* Customers
* Suppliers
* Employees
* Finance
* Analytics
* Automation

through a flexible and configurable architecture.

# Final Vision

StoqBook is designed to be more than a stock management application. Its architecture enables it to evolve into a complete Business Operating System (Business OS), where inventory, sales, purchasing, workforce management, reporting, automation, integrations, and AI work together through a modular, configurable, and enterprise-ready platform.
